# Lodestone's first site, archived 2026-11-14

These files are Lodestone's walkable site as it stood before the site rebuild (`docs/plans/lodestone-world.md` section 7,
`docs/plans/world-distinction-plan.md`): a flat canyon floor between two straight walls, a haul road down the middle and
six huts either side of it at x = +-24, with drum stacks, ore heaps and boulders filling the gaps.
