# Lodestone (`unit12`): Transition Metals — World Spec & Build Record

**World:** Lodestone (`unit12`, world 12 in road order), the iron canyons.
**Subject:** the transition metals: d-electron arrangements and ions, several charges, complex ions, their shapes and isomers, colour and magnetism. Beyond the AP exam throughout (owner decision 2026-11-11: followable by a 13-year-old, and "beyond the exam" appears nowhere in play text).
**Structure:** 6 benches (41 stages) + 1 boss (`boss-coil`, 5 phases) = 46 stages, 7 quest modules.
**Kit:** `lode` (`src/learn/table/kits/lode*.js`), extending the heat kit's cards, slots, trays, rails and plaques. Planners in `src/learn/engine/lodedata.js`.
**Status:** spec written 2026-11-13 (Phase 0). The build record is appended at the end as phases finish.

Sources this spec follows: `docs/teaching-pass/worlds/unit12-lodestone.md` (the plan session's four-bench plan), `docs/plans/learn-quests.md` World 12 (five quests of eight), `PRODUCT.md` "Building a Learn world". Where they disagree this spec says what it chose and why.

**Why six benches and not four.** The plan session cut the world to four benches of five or six stages; the world-build prompt asks for six to nine benches. Six benches are made by splitting the plan's `q1-dblock` (configurations and the 4s-first click) from a new `q2-charges` (successive ionization energies, the Fe²⁺/Mn²⁺ third-electron click, the definition of a transition metal), and the plan's `q2-complex` into `q3-complex` (ligands, the coordinate bond, coordination number, charge, chelates) and `q4-shapes` (octahedral, tetrahedral, square planar, cis/trans). Benches stay below eight stages where eight would pad (Redesign Hierarchy item 6): 6, 6, 8, 6, 8, 7.

**What is left out, and why.** Smelting, refining, rusting and passivation (learn-quests `q5-metals`): sacrificial protection is Brinewire `q2` stage 6, electrolysis is Brinewire `q6`, furnace reductions are Kettle; nothing in them is about d electrons. Atomic radii across the d-block: a plot read-off with no decision. Naming complexes (IUPAC): notation with no instrument; one bank problem. The tetrahedral splitting pattern and square planar d⁸ diamagnetism: one more split diagram each, logged as possible later stages.

## 4. Kit plan (`lode`)

Planners (`src/learn/engine/lodedata.js`, pure, shared by both views):

- `ELEMENTS` (K–Zn: Z, outer 4s/3d ground states as measured, CRC), `IE` (first eight successive ionization energies, kJ/mol, NIST ASD via CRC: Ca, Sc, Ti, V, Cr, Mn, Fe, Co, Ni), `removalOrder(el, startCharge)` (subshell of each successive electron: 4s, then 3d paired before single, then 3p).
- `levelsFor(mode, charge)`: dshell levels (atom: 4s 0, 3d +1; ion: 3d −0.6, 4s +0.8; glide by charge), pairing cost 0.3; `energyOf(levels, occ, P)`, `lowestOcc(levels, N, P)`, `movesTo(levels, occ)` (the electrons that could sit lower and where), `unpaired(occ)`.
- `splitLevels(gap)`: t2g at −0.4Δ, eg at +0.6Δ; Δ and pairing cost P in kJ/mol (P = 210, gaps narrow 120, wide 300 for the drawn ladder); `fillLowest(N, gap, P)`.
- `COMPLEXES`: formula, metal, charge, d count, ligands, geometry, λmax, colour seen, spin, unpaired (Housecroft & Sharpe; Shriver & Atkins).
- `photonKJ(nm)` = N_A·h·c/λ ÷ 1000; `bandOf(nm)` six colour bands; `COMPLEMENT`.
- `pull(unpaired)` ∝ n(n + 2) (spin-only moment squared).
- Complex charge `chargeOf(metal, ligands)`, site crowding by ligand size (H₂O, NH₃ six; Cl⁻ four; en three of two places), rotation group of the square (8) and octahedron (24) for `match`.

Fixtures (one file per family, registered through `lode-registry.js`): `dshell`, `iestrip`, `cplx`, `match`, `beaker`, `spec`, `cfield`, `dsplit`, `magnet`, `lodelead`. Pieces: `vial` (a sample tube that keeps its pull and its band), `elem` (element card). Heat kit cards (`note`, `num`), slots, trays, rails, plaques.

3D (`lode-3d.js`): `cplx` stands its ion and ligands up as balls and rods turned by its Turn dial; `magnet` stands two pole pieces and the hanging tube; `vial` is a glass tube with its solution's colour. ## 6. Pitfall review

Teaching leaks: idea in the reward — every reward above confirms what the stage showed; terms are introduced by briefings (subshell, ionization energy, transition metal, isomer, complementary colour, crystal field response, paramagnetic, pairing cost) or named on the reward of the stage that showed them (ligand, coordinate bond, coordination number, chelate, cis/trans, low/high spin). Decorative motion — every moving model is the thing acted on (ladder levels glide with the charge the student makes; bars appear per removal; ligands settle per tap; the band moves per ammonia press; vials swing per Switch On). Black box printing the property — the magnet prints a pull, not an unpaired count; the strip prints energies, never "oxidation state"; the spectrum prints a dark band, never "absorbs orange"; the `cfield` ladder is unlabelled. Plate-and-type — the one typed stage (`q5` s6) has the spectrum and the band it describes. Two voices — glossary bans "ring" (for ligands), "holds", "grabs", "wants", "prefers", "boxes" (say orbital), "pocket", "sticks". Anthropomorphic — "repels", "attracts", "costs energy" only. Convoluted answer forms — counts, cards, builds, taps; the typed figure is kJ/mol directly. Hint leaks — rung 3 gives method only; reviewed per stage. Prerequisites — configurations, ionization energy, lone pair, photon energy recapped in briefings; BUILDS_ON lists Lanternfall and Ligar quests. Owner requests built as opposites — the plan asked for a visual crystal-field view: built as `cfield`, not cards. Plate prints answer — the sealed drums print evidence only; checked in `lodestone-learn-checks.mjs`. Refusal states the rule — misses name readings ("the strip still shows blue"), reviewed. Tray names state answers — "Transition metal" trays come after the briefing defines it (discovered by the ion ladders); "Coloured/Colourless" are observations. Term before taught — glossary stage gates. Model not held — wrong models are on cards (last-in-first-out, IE rises across a row, one-from-each bond, flat hexagon, six placements, the metal's own colour, Hund always). Repeats — each stage carries a new case. Story — lead per bench, the drums are measured.

Operating: crowded rows — sites are taps on the picture, pieces are few (≤5 vials or cards, 60-unit spacing kept for cards). Dropped tool never joins — vials go into slots (`slot` drop by pointer, two-tap twin). Missed grab — engine-wide. Drop beside slot — engine rule. Dials — every dial has ≤ 6 detents. Invisible tool — vials are solid tubes. Control mentioned — captions on keys. Late control — taps set dials at once. Tiny text — painters use `F.size(…, min)` floors of 4.3 px units; must-read numbers at ≥ 2.2 units. Black pane — engine overlay.

Rendering: `c.label` metres — not used directly. Slab over display — no bezel slabs; `deckStyle` instrument frame only. Glass over text — vials stand in holders whose painted labels sit beside, not behind. Lining hides contents — no jars. Painted words under knobs — `dialIndent` 3.6 as Quicklight; dial rows sit at the top, content below 16.5 (Brinewire rule: content 11 units below a dial strip). Fonts — element letters in sans; true minus "−" and superscripts from Unicode only where the font has them (²⁺ ³⁺ ⁻ ⁴⁻ are in Quantico; checked in the browser). Drawn contents off puck — n/a. Plots too small — strips ≥ 60 × 30 units. Option objects dropping keys — every fixture builder lists its options explicitly and `verify:lodestone` checks the props reach the fixture.

Teeter/Verdigris/Brinewire additions: comparison trace before run — the second ion strip draws only after its press. Finite-particle stats — none. Zero in scientific form — no sci printing except the plate constants. Trays at right edge — trays ≤ x 128. Tiny card words — `note` cards for statements, `num` for numbers. Parallel builders — one file per fixture family; shared lists edited by one line. A start reading gives the answer — sealed stages hide the name and the meter. Neighbour table gives verdict — the drum plates print evidence only. Two builders one kind name — all kinds prefixed by family (`dshell`, `iestrip`...) and registered once. Float tolerance — typed tolerances carry margins. Window cannot show a small fraction — n/a. Caption longer than table — fixtures 30+ units from the right edge. Hidden dial with a Tour sentence — tap-set dials are `hidden` and the fixture Tour says `dials: false`. `sci()` rounding — not used.
