# Thornmere (`unit13`): Organic Chemistry — World Spec & Build Record

**World:** Thornmere (`unit13`, world 13 in road order), the tar swamps.
**Subject:** carbon compounds: chains and rings, double bonds, isomers, functional groups, how organic reactions happen as movements of electron pairs, and polymers. Beyond the AP exam throughout (owner decision 2026-11-11: followable by a 13-year-old, and "beyond the exam" appears nowhere in play text).
**Structure:** 6 benches of 8 stages (48) + 1 boss (`boss-carapace`, 5 phases) = 53 stages, 7 quest modules.
**Kit:** `thorn` (`src/learn/table/kits/thorn*.js`), extending the heat kit's cards, slots, trays, rails, plaques and markers. Planners and data in `src/learn/engine/thorndata.js`.
**Status:** spec written 2026-11-14 (Phase 0). The build record is appended at the end as phases finish.

Sources: `docs/teaching-pass/worlds/unit13-thornmere.md` (the plan session's five-bench, 28-stage plan), `docs/plans/learn-quests.md` World 13 (six quests of eight), `PRODUCT.md` "Building a Learn world", `docs/quests/q1-charge-gardens.md`, and the owner's note on this run. Where they disagree this spec says what it chose and why.

## 0. Decisions that part from the earlier plans

- **Electron movement is taught here, and it builds on Erebus (owner's note, 2026-11-14).** The earlier plans left mechanisms to the campaign and had Thornmere "name kinds of reaction and stop there". The owner's note for this run says the world needs intuition for electron movement and should build off Erebus. So every reaction in this world is shown, and where it is the idea is graded, as a pair of electrons moving from an electron-rich site to an electron-poor one, with the Charge Gardens' four intuitions: opposite charges attract, a carbon has four bond seats so one bond gives way when another arrives, crowding blocks a path, reactions run in steps. The world is not locked behind Erebus: `electron-rich`, `electron-poor` and `curved arrow` are recapped at first use (glossary `NEEDS` with primers, and a briefing in `q2` stage 4).
- **No college names.** PRODUCT principle 9 stands: nucleophile, electrophile, SN1, SN2, carbocation, leaving group and "mechanism" appear nowhere in play text. The words are *electron-rich site*, *electron-poor site*, *curved arrow*, and the reaction kinds *addition*, *substitution*, *condensation*, *hydrolysis*.
- **Six benches, not five.** The plan session folded reactions into the group and polymer benches. With electron movement in scope, reactions get their own bench (`q5-reactions`), between functional groups and polymers.
- **Eight stages per bench**, reached by adding the electron-movement stages, a typed worked figure (`q1`, `q6`) and sealed samples, not by padding.
- **Dropped from the plan session's list:** the fats-that-pack stage, the chiral pocket stage, the ether and haloalkane builds, the smell of esters. Reasons: each was a reading with no instrument behind it.

## 3. Stage tables

**How to read a row.** *Objective* is the prompt as built (≤ 25 words). *Evidence* is what on the bench lets a student who does not know the answer work it out. *Prints* is everything the bench shows as text or number on that stage, with the check that none of it is the answer. *Wrong → miss* gives the plausible mistakes and what each refusal names. *Reward* is the card's gist (built ≤ 40 words). `B:` is a briefing (≤ 2 sentences). Hints are written in the quest files to the ladder (1 where to look, 2 what to compare, 3 how to reason) and are read back with `node tools/teaching-report.mjs unit13 --read`; the audit records the check of each. Every stage is drawn flat (structural formulas are diagrams) except the twist and the mirror, which draw a projected 3D model that turns, because position in space is their idea.
