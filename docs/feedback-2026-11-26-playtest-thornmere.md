## In general for Thornmere

**Thornmere, whole world.** The story and the chemistry join up only at the very end and the join is wrong: the world is "find out what is under the plastic armor", the benches teach hydrocarbons, bromine, mirror forms, groups, reactions and polymers, and the boss's Case Pin announces "Thick lead plates sit under the plastic to block nuclear radiation." which none of the seven benches found or even touched. Either let a bench find the lead (a heavy, dense sample test on the armor) or change the opening question; at the end the world panel still reads "Find out what is under it." even with 7 of 7 cleared.
**Thornmere, question cards.** Five of the seven benches open with a story question that the first stage does not follow from: q1 (tar -> hydrogens on two carbons), q2 (the heater -> a double bond's hydrogens), q4 (a drum eating steel -> boiling points of ethanol), q5 (acid + alcohol links -> hydroxide on bromoethane), boss (weight -> hydrogens of a wax). Write each card so that its answer is what the last stage finds, and open stage 1 with the sentence that bridges them.
**Thornmere, electron-pushing controls.** Four benches (q2, q4, q5, q6) use "tap a pair, tap an atom, press Move Electrons". The pair dots are 3 px, lone pairs sit beside bond pairs, the molecule rotates, a miss leaves the old banner and an old selection ring on screen, and "Move Electrons" with nothing chosen does nothing and says nothing. Make pairs at least 12 px with a halo on the one that matters, stop the rotation when a pair is selected, clear the banner on a new tap, and say "Choose a pair and an atom first". The hint rung 1 on these stages is the same how-to sentence each time ("Tap a pair of electrons, then the atom it moves to, and press "Move Electrons".").
**Thornmere, tours.** The tour's stock steps are wrong for many stages: "ELECTRON VIEW: Tap an atom, bond, or electron pair to select or push electrons." appears on stages with no electrons (q3 s1, q4 s1, q5 s5 and others), "MODEL BOARD: Tap a dashed ring to place C..." appears on benches without rings or with fixed carbons (q1 s7, q2 s1), and "TEST RIG: Drag a sample card into the slot and press the test key." refers to a key that is a named button ("Add Bromine Water", "Heat", "Twist").
