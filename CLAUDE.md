# Avalon — Engineering Reference

Avalon is the UHS Chemistry Club's competition portal for middle- and high-school students (ages 12–17). Core doctrine: **teach chemistry clearly, intuitively, and hands-on, without giving away answers.**

## Codebase Map

- `src/main.js` — App bootstrapper (stage, HUD, hash router).
- `src/learn/table/near.js` — puts a table's instrument nearest the player and its options beyond it (bands swapped at `createTable`; details in `docs/engineering/engine-and-worlds.md`, "The instrument stands nearest the player"). `window.__lqStage(n)` (dev builds) opens stage n of the open bench.
- `src/learn/table/balance.js` — centres a row of loose pieces in the bare run of its line and opens touching pieces apart (after the near swap; `layout: 'as-drawn'` skips it). `notes.js` keeps how-to lines off a label's line and centred. Rules in `docs/engineering/engine-and-worlds.md`, "Benches look laid out, not dropped".
- `src/learn/table/glide.js` — a dragged dial's picture rides with the pointer between steps (the value still snaps to the nearest step as it moves, and the picture settles on it when let go). `gestures.js` sets it from track drags (`dialTo`) and zone drags (`tapDrag`); track knobs are drawn from `knobFrac` (`table2d.js`, `table3d.js` `glided`, with visible 3D tick marks for discrete dials), and kit pictures dragged directly read `gliding`/`glideValue`: the press piston (`matter-window.js`), the vessel piston (`pans-stress.js`), the fulcrum (`mass.js`), and the heat kit's well, shelves, plot and curve (`heat-read.js` reads take `shown`, `heat-motion.js` `glideHand`). Printed readings and grading always use the snapped value. A new table mid-drag (every step is one) does not drop a dial, zone or stick drag (`TableGestures.tableChanged`, called by both views' `setState`); a new stage or rebuilt board does. In `src/learn/table/model.js`, `kit.returnFree` snaps free drops back to `home` and `zone.single` returns the displaced card to `home` on single-card trays; in `src/learn/engine/bench3d.js`, empty-table drags do not rotate or tilt the camera while leaning over a bench; in `src/learn/engine/frame.js`, table-mode stage lamps show stage numbers and refusal banners stay pinned above open hints; in `src/learn/table/kits/pans-3d.js`, `dressRecorded` accepts `{ flat: true }` (used by `matter.js`) so 3D molecules rest on the plate without lifting vertically out of cylinders/tanks/flasks.
- `src/learn/table/kits/atoms.js` loose Lewis board (`looseSlateParts`, Ligar `q4-dots`) — atoms start apart, bond when one is dragged onto another, and re-pose after every action (`settleLooseSlates` from `lewis.js` `poseDrawing`: bonds and lone pairs spaced evenly round each atom); `q5-resonance` and `boss-warden` keep the fixed slate (clickable `markable` atoms via `{ flip }` on CO and CO₂). In `src/learn/engine/atom3d.js`, bonding-scale atoms (`opts.shells`) render only valence electrons around a generic nucleus ball, with hopping ionic transfer electrons and non-overlapping fixed covalent pairs (including double/triple bonds). The Tour can play a stage's own solution with a hand (`tour-demo.js` `stageDemo`, now including `join` moves; `kits/atoms-tour-demos.js`).
- `src/learn/table/solids.js` + `kits/solid-claims.js` — at 3D On, bench pictures of atoms, ions and molecules (Ligar to Cinder Halo; not Tallow or Lanternfall) stand up as ball-and-stick solids read from the kit's own flat painter, and the flat picture is painted without them; 3D Off keeps them flat. ## Teaching Doctrine (Learn Track)

Full rules in `PRODUCT.md`. Non-negotiables:
- **Question upfront & focused:** Prompt states what to find (≤25 words, no operational instructions). One decision per stage; solvable from bench evidence alone.
- **Visible cause & real terms:** Models/particles respond directly to student action (heat via particle motion, never as a particle). Use real terms glossed on first use (`src/learn/glossary/`); no euphemisms ("push", "room") or anthropomorphic atom motives ("happy", "wants").
- **Tabletop as the answer:** Constructed on the table (2D / 3D Off parity), not MCQs (unless reading-only). Every drag has a two-tap twin.
- **Predict first, test second:** Commit hypothesis before revealing instruments (`src/learn/table/kits/commit-gate.js`); predictions are never graded.
- **Hints & rewards:** 3 one-sentence hint rungs (where to look → what to compare → how to reason; never the answer). Reward card (≤40 words) confirms what was seen; never introduces new concepts.
- **Trials, not combat:** Ordinary misses are "missed trials" with reasons. Foes are dispersed, never killed; only bosses fight.

## Hard Engineering Rules

- **Learn track pays no XP:** `src/learn/` never calls `session.addXp`, `api.gradeStage`, or `api.completeQuest`. Practice gates nothing and pays no XP.
- **Practice bank:** All practice problems live in `src/learn/bank/<worldId>.json` (2 per bench; 1 worked problem for computational benches) plus `src/learn/bank/worked-deep.json` (22 multi-concept `AP` and `Olympiad` worked problems with `strategy`, step `beat`, and `takeaway` cues, playable in `src/ui/worked-problem.js` with the built-in `calculator.js` and filterable in the Practice / Drill Deck, which pulls only from completed quests/worlds on a full-screen black backdrop with calculator access and no streaks or Daily Set); every `calc` expression is verified by `verify:learn` and `verify-drills.mjs`.
- **Persistence & guest mode:** Google Sheets is master; `localStorage` is cache (`src/shared/statemerge.js`). Sync failures throw `BACKEND_BUSY` without logging out. Use `session.startGuest()` for local/automated checks.
- **Physical honesty:** Flat things (spectra, graphs) drawn flat; spatial concepts (orbitals, lattices, molecules) in 3D. What is drawn is what is graded.
- **3D & performance:** No physical overlaps (`tools/lib/overlap.mjs`, 0.06 m tolerance). Pointer is never locked. Fixed pooled `PointLight`s only (`src/three/lamp-pool.js`). Static props use `mergeStatic`/`consolidateStatic`; keep shadows resting when idle (`shadow-pace.js`); no `backdrop-filter` over the live canvas. DPR capped 1.5 (paced down on planets); nothing new may compile or upload textures on the frame it first appears (`warmWorld`, `Crew.warm`).
- **UI lifecycle:** Dedicated `.learn-quest-2d` layout for 3D Off. `mountConsole` must be the final statement in Charge Gardens stage render.

## Aesthetic — "SCOURED PLATE"

- **Look:** Obsidian/titanium (`--plate-000...600`), warm filament light, amber as signal only, Quantico (JetBrains Mono fallback), 1px bezel seams.
- **Banned:** Neon/LED blooms (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px` except true indicator lamps), cyan, glassmorphism, candy gradients, rounded corners (except `--radius-full`), emoji in markup, fake status indicators, feature-card grids.
- **Copy:** No marketing or promotional text; only necessary labels, rules, errors, instruction, and debriefs.

## Commands & Hygiene

- `npm run dev` — Local Vite dev server (`:3000`) with API proxy.
- `npm run build` — Production build into `dist/`.
- `npm run verify` — Full verification suite (`tools/verify-all.mjs`) + build.
- **Aids:** `node tools/teaching-report.mjs <unitNN> [quest] [--read|--totals]`, `node tools/perf-worlds.mjs`, `node tools/world-shape.mjs`.
- **Testing:** Agents do NOT test in the browser unless the user says so (asking for the `playtest-review` skill counts); verify via `npm run verify` and `npm run build`.
- **Opening a bench in the browser (when allowed):** use `localhost:3000` (`.claude/launch.json` config `avalon-3000`). 1) Continue as Guest and finish onboarding once (join a guild, hold the oath button, Mission Briefing); if it loops, set `session.player.team_id = 'fire'` via `await import('/src/session.js')`. 2) **First mark every quest complete, persisted** — `session.markLearnQuestComplete(w.id, q.id)` for every live quest in `WORLDS` (`/src/learn/curriculum.js`); setting `session.learn` in memory is lost on reload and the bench route then bounces to the world. 3) **Jump straight to the bench:** set `location.hash = '#/learn/<unitNN>/<questId>'` and **reload the page**. Changing the hash without a reload while aboard the ship starts a flight (`router.tryVoyage`) and lands on the world instead; the first route of a page load stands on the world at the bench. 4) `window.__lqStage(n)` (dev) opens stage n of the open bench.
- **Retire by archiving:** Never delete functional code, docs, or checks; move to `archive/<date>/` or `docs/archive/<date>/` and disconnect live wiring.
